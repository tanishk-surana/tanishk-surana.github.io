Go-to-market strategy for Orangewood Labs

Orangewood Labs builds affordable, AI-programmable robotic arms for small and medium enterprises. The question was which applications were worth commercialising, and on what evidence.

We built a decision framework gating each use case on payback tolerance, deployment feasibility, buyer ownership and repeatability, then ran four candidates through it. The core finding was that SMEs buy automation to reduce operational instability, not to innovate, so the winning position is predictable deployment rather than hardware sophistication. We recommended machine tending and industrial pick-and-place as primary focus, visual inspection selectively, and airport bartending as pilot only.
